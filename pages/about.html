About InternLink

Connecting Students, Companies & Colleges

InternLink is a college internship management platform designed to simplify the internship journey for students, companies, and faculty members.

Students can explore internship opportunities, manage applications, track attendance, and monitor their progress. Companies can manage internship opportunities and student applications, while faculty members can monitor student progress.
